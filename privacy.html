Privacy Policy

Home Assistant Backup · Last updated: 18 October 2026

Home Assistant Backup ("the app") is a personal, non-commercial Google Cloud OAuth client used by a private Home Assistant installation to store backups in the user's own Google Drive. This policy explains what Google user data the app accesses and how it is handled.

1. Data the app accesses

  • Google Drive files created by the app, through the drive.file scope only. The app cannot read, list or change any other files in your Google Drive.
  • OAuth tokens issued by Google after you sign in, so Home Assistant can keep uploading backups without asking you to sign in each time.

The app does not request your contacts, email, calendar, location or any other Google data.

2. How the data is used

The app uses this access only to upload Home Assistant backup files to your Google Drive, and to list, download and delete those backup files (for example to restore a backup or remove old ones). It does not use the data for advertising, profiling, analytics or any other purpose.

3. Storage

Backup files are stored in your own Google Drive. OAuth tokens are stored only on your own Home Assistant server. The app has no separate server or database, so no copy of your data is kept anywhere else.

4. Sharing

Google user data is not sold, shared with or transferred to any third party.

5. Limited Use

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

6. Retention and deletion

Backup files stay in your Google Drive until you or Home Assistant delete them. You can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, or by removing the Google Drive integration in Home Assistant. Revoking access deletes nothing in your Drive; you can delete the backup files yourself.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.
